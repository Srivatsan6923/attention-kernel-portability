A dispatch-verified study of prefill and KV-cache decode across Ampere, Ada and Hopper.

1Summary

2Question

Attention libraries expose several optimized paths, but the requested API does not always identify the kernel that executes, and a ranking measured on one GPU need not hold on another. This study asks when the fastest attention backend changes across hardware and workload regime, why it changes, and whether a lightweight selector can predict it.

Do rankings transfer across GPUs?Matched-cell winner change and rank correlation

            7Decode and KV cache

            Cache traffic per decode step is 2 · B · Lkv · Hkv · D · s bytes per layer, where B is batch, Lkv the cached length, Hkv the number of key/value heads, D the head dimension and s the bytes per element. Grouped-query attention reduces Hkv and the traffic falls in proportion.
