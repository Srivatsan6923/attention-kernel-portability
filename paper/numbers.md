### Same-architecture control (the noise floor)

L40 and L40S are both sm89, 142 SMs, 96 MiB L2, with measured bandwidth 644.4 and 645.3 GB/s -- **0.1% apart**. They still disagree: Spearman **0.929** and **3.02%** practical inversions over 101 shared cells and 1953 implementation pairs. **No cross-architecture inversion rate at or below this floor is evidence of anything.**

Both devices ran **prefill only**, so this floor is calibrated for prefill and is not established for decode.

Pairs at or below the floor: A10 vs L40 (1.75%). Those are not usable as portability evidence.

`inversions.parquet` stores `pairs_examined` as a running counter written only onto inverting pairs, so its maximum undercounts the true denominator slightly. The denominators above are recomputed exactly from `cells.parquet`; they differ from the stored maxima by at most 5 pairs (<0.40%), which moves no rate in the third decimal.

## 9. Hardware-aware selector

A depth-4 decision tree over (cc, measured bandwidth, is_decode, log2 N, log2 B, head_dim, GQA ratio, is_bf16), **fit on A100 + H100 and tested on the devices it never saw** (A10, L40, L40S). The split is by hardware, not a random split of one grid, because the question is whether the rule transfers to an unseen GPU.

**Lead with regret, not accuracy.** Accuracy asks whether the tree names the exact winner; regret asks what it costs when it does not. Several implementations sit within noise of each other in most cells, so accuracy understates the rule badly.

## 10. Stability

The unit of variation is the **per-process median**, not the individual rep: reps inside one process share clocks, allocator state and thermal point, so they are not independent draws. CV = std/mean over process repeats (ddof=1, five processes being a sample of the machine's process-to-process behaviour). Relative CI width = (hi - lo) / (2 x median) from a 2000-draw cluster bootstrap over process medians.

| GPU | regime | triples | triples with >1 process | median process repeats | median CV | p95 CV | median rel. CI width | p95 rel. CI width |

| A10 | decode | 955 | 955 | 5 | 0.0077 | 0.0430 | 0.0058 | 0.0329 |
| A10 | prefill | 916 | 916 | 5 | 0.0125 | 0.0932 | 0.0096 | 0.0718 |
| A100 | decode | 966 | 966 | 5 | 0.0511 | 0.3066 | 0.0397 | 0.2584 |
| A100 | prefill | 724 | 248 | 1 | 0.0378 | 0.2357 | 0.0000 | 0.1129 |

**663 of 8642 usable triples have a single process repeat**, so they have no CV at all and a bootstrap CI of width 0. They are 476 on A100 prefill, 186 on L40S prefill and 1 on L40 prefill. Any A100-prefill or L40S CI figure is dominated by these and is an artefact, not a precision measurement.

Safe headline, restricted to the device-regimes that genuinely have 5 process repeats: between-process CV is a median **0.0109** on A10 (n=1871 triples), **0.0511** on A100 decode (n=966) and **0.0260** on H100 (n=2474).

H100 is the noisiest device in the set at the tail -- p95 CV **0.461** and p95 relative CI width **0.347**, against 0.070 and 0.053 on A10. That tail is exactly what the 1.10x practical-inversion threshold exists to survive.

### C. Wording discipline these numbers do not license

- **FlashAttention does not remove quadratic FLOPs.** Nothing here measures arithmetic; the `tflops` column is an *algorithmic* FLOP count divided by measured time. FA reduces materialisation and memory traffic -- exact attention still does quadratic arithmetic.
- Every claim is scoped to a **(kernel, GPU, regime, software-distribution snapshot)**. Exactly one torch / flash-attn / flashinfer combination was measured; a different wheel is a different experiment, not a replication.
- The packaging hypothesis -- that sm_86 and sm_89 devices execute sm_80 cubins under CUDA minor-version binary compatibility -- is **consistent with** the pattern of P4-fa2 wins and losses. No rebuild was performed, so it is not shown to **cause** anything, and the provenance artefact backing it is not in this directory (section A).
- **No speedup appears in this document without its absolute latency**, and none should appear in the paper without one either.
- A winner-flip rate is meaningless without its **n_gpus and n_cells**. Rates from different device counts are not comparable.
