### Same-architecture control (the noise floor)

L40 and L40S are both sm89, 142 SMs, 96 MiB L2, with measured bandwidth 644.4 and 644.4 GB/s -- **0.0% apart**. They still disagree: Spearman **0.929** and **1.66%** practical inversions over 336 shared cells and 4755 implementation pairs. **No cross-architecture inversion rate at or below this floor is evidence of anything.**

Both devices ran **prefill only**, so this floor is calibrated for prefill and is not established for decode.

Pairs at or below the floor: none. Those are not usable as portability evidence.

`inversions.parquet` stores `pairs_examined` as a running counter written only onto inverting pairs, so its maximum undercounts the true denominator slightly. The denominators above are recomputed exactly from `cells.parquet`; they differ from the stored maxima by at most 5 pairs (<0.14%), which moves no rate in the third decimal.

## 7. Status taxonomy

These are **kinds of outcome, not severities**. A row is exactly one of them and they are not ordered: an UNSUPPORTED row is not a worse OK, and an OOM_PREDICTED row is not a failed OOM.

| status | meaning | written when | n rows | share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OK | The cell was built, ran, passed the correctness gate for its equivalence class, and was timed. **Only these rows enter any ranking.** | the measurement completed | 54213 | 74.0% |
| UNSUPPORTED | The implementation declined this (config, device). Either the kernel does not exist for this compute capability or shape, **or** the config lies outside the strip this implementation is deliberately run on. Not a failure and not a crash. | `impl.supports()` false, `impl_applies()` false, `NotImplementedError`, or a `RuntimeError` naming 'No available kernel' / 'not supported' | 16326 | 22.3% |

## 9. Hardware-aware selector

A depth-4 decision tree over (cc, measured bandwidth, is_decode, log2 N, log2 B, head_dim, GQA ratio, is_bf16), **fit on A100 + H100 and tested on the devices it never saw** (A10, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, L40, L40S). The split is by hardware, not a random split of one grid, because the question is whether the rule transfers to an unseen GPU.

**Lead with regret, not accuracy.** Accuracy asks whether the tree names the exact winner; regret asks what it costs when it does not. Several implementations sit within noise of each other in most cells, so accuracy understates the rule badly.

## 10. Stability

### C. Wording discipline these numbers do not license

- **FlashAttention does not remove quadratic FLOPs.** Nothing here measures arithmetic; the `tflops` column is an *algorithmic* FLOP count divided by measured time. FA reduces materialisation and memory traffic -- exact attention still does quadratic arithmetic.
- Every claim is scoped to a **(kernel, GPU, regime, software-distribution snapshot)**. Exactly one torch / flash-attn / flashinfer combination was measured; a different wheel is a different experiment, not a replication.
- The packaging hypothesis -- that sm_86 and sm_89 devices execute sm_80 cubins under CUDA minor-version binary compatibility -- is **consistent with** the pattern of P4-fa2 wins and losses. No rebuild was performed, so it is not shown to **cause** anything, and the provenance artefact backing it is not in this directory (section A).
- **No speedup appears in this document without its absolute latency**, and none should appear in the paper without one either.
- A winner-flip rate is meaningless without its **n_gpus and n_cells**. Rates from different device counts are not comparable.
